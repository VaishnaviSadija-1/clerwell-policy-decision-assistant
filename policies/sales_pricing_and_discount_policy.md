# Sales Pricing and Discount Policy

Version 4.0 | Effective 10 August 2026

## Pricing

- Starter: INR 1,200 per user per month for 10-49 users.
- Growth: INR 950 per user per month for 50-199 users.
- Enterprise: INR 750 per user per month for 200 or more users. Implementation fees are scoped separately.

## Required information

A quote request must include customer name, user count, billing frequency, requested start date, and requested discount percentage.

## Discount approval

- Discounts up to and including 10% may be applied by Sales without additional approval.
- Discounts above 10% and up to 25% require Sales Manager approval.
- Discounts above 25% require Sales Manager and Finance approval.

## Non-standard terms

Custom liability, indemnity, governing-law, or termination language requires Legal review. Custom security commitments, data residency, or customer-specific SLA commitments require Security review.

Do not invent taxes, implementation fees, contractual terms, or service commitments.
