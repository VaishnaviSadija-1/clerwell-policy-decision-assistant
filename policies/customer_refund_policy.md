# Customer Refund Policy

Version 3.1 | Effective 10 August 2026

## Required information

A refund request must include account email, invoice number, purchase date, amount paid, payment method, and the number of processed workflows.

## Standard eligibility

- A standard subscription is eligible for refund within 14 calendar days of purchase when usage is below 20 processed workflows.
- An eligible refund up to INR 50,000 may be handled by Customer Support without additional approval.
- An eligible refund above INR 50,000 requires Finance Manager approval.

## Exceptions

Requests made after 14 calendar days, duplicate-charge claims, chargebacks, and goodwill exceptions require Support Lead and Finance review. The application must not promise that an exception will be approved.

## Processing

An approved refund is returned to the original payment method within 7-10 business days.
