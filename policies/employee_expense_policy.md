# Employee Expense and Travel Policy

Version 1.4 | Effective 1 July 2026

## Required information

Every claim must include employee ID, expense date, amount, currency, business purpose, and cost center. A receipt is required for expenses above INR 1,000.

## Approval thresholds

- Expenses up to INR 5,000 require reporting-manager approval.
- Expenses from INR 5,001 through INR 25,000 require department-head approval.
- Expenses above INR 25,000 require department-head and Finance approval.

## Travel

International travel requires advance department-head and Finance approval regardless of value.

## Exclusions

Alcohol, personal entertainment, traffic fines, and personal travel upgrades are not reimbursable.
