# Employee Leave Policy

Version 2.0 | Effective 1 August 2026

## Required information

A leave request must include the employee ID, leave type, start date, end date, number of working days, available leave balance, and reporting manager.

## Annual leave

- Employees receive 18 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
- Annual leave may not exceed the employee's available balance.
- Up to 3 consecutive working days requires reporting-manager approval.
- 4 to 7 consecutive working days requires reporting-manager and HR approval.
- More than 7 consecutive working days requires department-head and HR approval.

## Sick leave

- Sick leave of 1 or 2 consecutive working days requires reporting-manager approval.
- Sick leave longer than 2 consecutive working days requires a medical certificate and HR approval.

## Release freeze

Any leave that overlaps a published release-freeze period requires department-head approval in addition to the approvals above.

## Privacy

Medical details must not be repeated in generated drafts, decision explanations, or activity logs.
